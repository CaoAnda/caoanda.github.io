# Publication figure sources

These previews reproduce figures from the authors' papers, without redrawing their contents. Each homepage preview links to its larger image.

- `enmp-framework.png`: Evolutionary Negative Module Pruning for Better LoRA Merging, arXiv:2604.17753v1, `figs/framework.pdf` from the [LaTeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/2604.17753v1). Full framework figure rendered at 1200 px width.
- `jpa-method.png`: Jailbreaking Prompt Attack, arXiv:2404.02928v4, `img/main.pdf` from the [LaTeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/2404.02928v4). Panel (a), the algorithm flow, extracted at 144 dpi; panel (b) is omitted.
- `mamba-comparison.png`: Vision Mamba Mender, Figure 5, page 8 of the [paper hosted on the official project website](https://vision-mamba-mender.github.io/static/pdfs/paper.pdf). All four comparison panels and their shared legend extracted at 216 dpi. A valid arXiv source link was not available on the project page.

Retrieved 2026-09-29. The figures belong to their respective authors and remain subject to the original publications' licenses.
