I am a PhD student at the College of Computer Science, Zhejiang University, working in the VIPA Lab co-advised by Prof. Mingli Song and Prof. Jie Song. My research interests lie in **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and **Model Merging** &mdash; with a focus on developing efficient and effective methods for combining multiple fine-tuned models, particularly for multi-task learning scenarios.
