I am a PhD student at the College of Computer Science, Zhejiang University, working in the VIPA Lab co-advised by Prof. Mingli Song and Prof. Jie Song. My research interests lie in **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and **Model Merging** &mdash; with a focus on developing efficient and effective methods for combining multiple fine-tuned models, particularly for multi-task learning scenarios.

## Education

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Zhejiang University (2024 &ndash; present), co-advised by Prof. Mingli Song and Prof. Jie Song
- **M.S. in Artificial Intelligence**, Zhejiang University, School of Software Technology (2022 &ndash; 2024), VIPA Lab, advised by Prof. Mingli Song
- **B.E. in Intelligent Science and Technology**, Zhejiang Sci-Tech University, Qixin Honors School (2018 &ndash; 2022)

**Research Interests:** LLM, Model Merging, Deep Learning, Computer Vision
